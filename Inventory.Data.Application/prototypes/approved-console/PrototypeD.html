Reporting Dashboard

Wonder Group has no central ERP. A synthetic unified inventory ledger plus a purchase-order (PO) table in BigQuery serve as the sub-ledger Accounting uses to book the general ledger at month-end. Each morning a batch job validates the prior day's data, flags errors, auto-creates JIRA tickets routed to the right team, tracks turnaround against SLA, spots recurring errors, and auto-closes tickets once Data Engineering fixes the underlying table. Start here for the health picture — then click any tile or chart to drill into the underlying exceptions.

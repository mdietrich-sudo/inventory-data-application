Wonder Inventory Data-Quality Console

Wonder Group has no central ERP. A synthetic unified inventory ledger plus a purchase-order (PO) table in BigQuery serve as the sub-ledger that Accounting uses to book the general ledger at month-end. Each morning a batch job validates the prior day's data, flags errors, and auto-creates JIRA tickets routed to the right team and person. It tracks how long tickets take to resolve (turnaround / SLA), spots recurring errors, and auto-closes tickets once Data Engineering fixes the underlying table and the issue stops reproducing. This console is where staff across the company triage those exceptions and where managers track performance.

How the daily pipeline works

  1. Ingest & validate: overnight batch reads the prior day's unified_ledger & po_table and runs configured rules (NOT_NULL, REFERENTIAL, RECONCILIATION, RANGE).
  2. Flag & route: each failing row becomes an exception and is routed by error type to a team / default assignee, with a JIRA ticket auto-created in project WIQ.
  3. Triage: owners work tickets in the Exception Workbench — reassign, override routing, add notes, open in JIRA.
  4. Track: managers watch turnaround & SLA breaches per team / person and recurring-error fingerprints.
  5. Auto-close: when a fixed issue no longer reproduces on the next run, its ticket is auto-closed by the validator.
